Where We Invest

We back founders in five sectors where deep and durable change is taking shape.

Luxury FashionRobotics & AutomationHealthTechFinTechTravelTech
01 / 05

Luxury Fashion

Luxury is moving from heritage alone to heritage with relevance. We back brands that pair real craft with a clear identity and a direct bond with their customers. Our roots in diamonds and jewellery give us an insider’s view of this world.

What we look for
  • A distinctive design language
  • Strong craft and supply chains
  • Brands built for global appeal
02 / 05

Robotics & Automation

Industry is being rebuilt around machines that see, move and decide. We back teams turning that capability into safer, faster and more precise operations.

What we look for
  • Deep technical teams
  • Clear industrial use cases
  • Hardware with a path to scale
03 / 05

HealthTech

Care is shifting closer to the patient and earlier in the journey. We back solutions that widen access, improve outcomes and lower the cost of good care.

What we look for
  • Clinical credibility
  • Measurable patient outcomes
  • Models that reach underserved markets
04 / 05

FinTech

Money is becoming simpler, faster and more open. We back platforms that bring transparency and fairness to how people and businesses save, pay and borrow.

What we look for
  • Trust and regulatory readiness
  • A real customer problem solved
  • Infrastructure others can build on
05 / 05

TravelTech

The way people plan and experience the world is being redesigned. We back products that make travel more personal, seamless and rewarding.

What we look for
  • Clear gains for the traveller
  • Strong partner networks
  • Businesses built across borders
