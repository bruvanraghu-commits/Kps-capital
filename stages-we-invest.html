Venture Capital
Stages We Invest

From first cheque to scale

Every company moves through stages. Each one asks something different of founders and their capital. Here is how the journey unfolds, step by step.

Step 1 · Idea stage

Pre-Seed

Turn an idea into an initial business concept.

Where capital goes

Founders, prototypes, market research and early product development.

Who typically invests

Angel investors, startup accelerators and pre-seed funds.

Example

A founder sets out to build an AI-powered education app.

Step 2 · Product development

Seed

Build the first product and validate customer demand.

Where capital goes

MVP development, hiring, customer acquisition and product testing.

Who typically invests

Seed-stage VC firms, angel investors and micro VCs.

Example

The app launches its first version and gains its first 1,000 users.

Step 3 · Business model validation

Series A

Build a repeatable business model and grow revenue.

Where capital goes

Product improvement, sales teams, marketing and operational systems.

Who typically invests

Institutional VC firms.

Example

With paying customers in hand, the company raises funds to expand across India.

Step 4 · Scaling

Series B

Expand a proven business rapidly.

Where capital goes

New markets, hiring at scale, technology infrastructure and acquisitions.

Who typically invests

Growth-oriented VC firms and larger institutional investors.

Example

The app expands to several countries and serves millions of learners.

Step 5 · Expansion and maturity

Series C and beyond

Fund major expansion and prepare for a public listing or acquisition.

Where capital goes

International growth, acquisitions, new business lines and market leadership.

Who typically invests

Late-stage VC firms, growth equity funds and institutional investors.

Example

The company acquires competitors and prepares for an IPO.

Wherever you are on the journey, we would like to hear from you.
